Install the PlusSizeBeauty App

Enjoy our fashion, beauty, skin care, hair care and shopping pages from an app icon on your device.

If your browser does not offer an Install button, use the instructions below.

Windows desktop (Microsoft Edge)

  1. Open this page in Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the three dots (…) at the top right.
  3. Select Apps → Install this site as an app.
  4. Choose Install. Pin the app to your taskbar or Start menu if offered.

Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
