Fashion that works with you.

Build outfits around comfort, fit, proportion and your personal taste—not rules about making yourself look smaller.

Start with fit

A size label is only a starting point. Prioritize how the garment sits at the shoulders, waist, hips and bust, and use tailoring when it makes a favorite piece work better.

Build reliable outfits

Create a few dependable combinations you can repeat: a strong top with jeans, a dress with a favorite layer, or trousers with a comfortable knit.

Use trends selectively

Try trends because you enjoy them. Keep the pieces that make getting dressed easier and skip the ones that do not serve your wardrobe.

A practical plus-size shopping checklist

1. Take measurements. Measure your bust, natural waist and fullest hip with a flexible tape measure. Compare them with each brand's current size chart; don't rely on the number on the label.

2. Read the fabric description. Stretch knits, woven cotton, denim and structured tailoring fit differently. Check whether the garment has stretch, lining or adjustable features.

3. Start with the area that is hardest to fit. Choose a garment that fits comfortably there, then tailor other areas if needed.

4. Look for outfit versatility. Try a dress with sneakers or boots, trousers with a tee or blouse, or a denim jacket over a favorite look.

5. Check return details. Know the return window and whether sale items are final before ordering.
