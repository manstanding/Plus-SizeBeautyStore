Before you buy

Clothing: Compare your measurements to the garment's size chart, read fabric details, and check returns. Sizes differ between brands.

Foundation: Consider undertone and finish, and test a shade in natural light where possible.

Skincare: Start with a gentle cleanser, moisturizer and broad-spectrum sunscreen. Introduce new products gradually and patch-test if your skin is sensitive.

Budget: Make a short list of what you actually need. A well-chosen basic often does more than several impulse purchases.
