FOR INDEPENDENT & GROWING BRANDS

Let's help your business be discovered.

Plus Size Beauty welcomes conversations with small and medium-sized businesses in fashion, beauty, hair, skincare, accessories and confidence-focused services. We can explore a feature that introduces your work to our readers.

Ways we can collaborate

Business spotlight

Introduce your brand story, what makes your business different and how customers can find you.

Product or service feature

Share photographs and useful details of a product or service relevant to our readers, subject to editorial review.

Local business discovery

Help readers discover an independent boutique, beauty professional or neighborhood business when our travels bring us together.

Who we're looking to meet

Inclusive clothing shops, stylists, makeup artists, skincare and hair professionals, accessories makers, wellness businesses, and independent brands that genuinely serve and respect fuller-bodied women.

We value clear product information, real photographs, honest claims and a welcoming customer experience.

How it works

1. Introduce your business

Share your business name, website or social profile, location, and what you would like people to discover.

2. Discuss a fit

We'll review whether your products or services fit our audience and discuss any proposed scope, timing, or fee before agreeing to anything.

3. Approve the details

Any sponsored placement will be clearly labeled. Photos and descriptions require appropriate permission to use.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a large business?

No. Independent and growing businesses are welcome to reach out.

Can I discuss a feature without paying upfront?

Yes. An inquiry is not a purchase or a commitment. Any paid work requires a separate written agreement.

Will a feature guarantee sales?

No. We cannot promise traffic, sales, rankings or results.

Can I submit photographs?

Yes, if you own them or have permission to authorize their use. Please contact us before sending large files.
