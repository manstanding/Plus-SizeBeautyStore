Beauty on your terms.

Makeup should be a tool for expression, not a requirement. Start with the products and routines that make you feel polished, playful or simply more like yourself.

Simple everyday routine

A basic routine can be moisturizer, SPF, complexion product where wanted, brows, mascara and a lip product. Add or subtract freely.

Choose products by need

Shop by skin type, finish, wear time and budget instead of chasing every new launch.

Color is for everyone

Bold lipstick, shimmer and bright color do not belong to one face shape, age or body size. Wear what you enjoy.

Build a routine that works for you

Morning: Cleanse gently if needed, moisturize, and apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Reapply sunscreen according to the product directions.

Complexion: Choose a foundation or tint by skin type, undertone, preferred coverage and finish. Test a small amount near your jaw in natural light when possible.

Eyes and lips: A brow product, mascara and a lip color can be a complete look. Try brighter colors whenever you feel like it.

Evening: Remove makeup, cleanse and moisturize. Introduce one new skincare product at a time so it's easier to notice irritation.

Shopping wisely: Compare price per ounce, shade range, return policy and reviews. Expensive does not automatically mean better.
