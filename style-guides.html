Easy style guides.

Use these principles as options, not restrictions. The best guide is the one that helps you get dressed with less stress.

Proportion, not hiding

If an outfit feels off, experiment with where tops and jackets end, where a waistline sits, and how volume is balanced.

Create a color plan

A small group of coordinating neutrals plus a few favorite colors makes mixing outfits easier.

Comfort counts

Shoes, fabrics and undergarments affect how long an outfit stays comfortable. Style should survive your real day.
