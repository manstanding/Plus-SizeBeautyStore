Easy style guides.

Use these principles as options, not restrictions. The best guide is the one that helps you get dressed with less stress.

Proportion, not hiding

If an outfit feels off, experiment with where tops and jackets end, where a waistline sits, and how volume is balanced.

Create a color plan

A small group of coordinating neutrals plus a few favorite colors makes mixing outfits easier.

Comfort counts

Shoes, fabrics and undergarments affect how long an outfit stays comfortable. Style should survive your real day.

Three outfits to try this week

Everyday: Straight-leg jeans, a comfortable tee, an open button-down and supportive sneakers. Try tucking just the front of the tee if you like that look.

Work or dinner: Wide-leg trousers, a fitted or draped top, a longline jacket and a favorite necklace. Adjust the jacket length to suit your proportions.

Weekend: A midi dress, denim jacket and sandals or boots. Add a belt only if you enjoy a defined waist.

Make shopping easier

Keep a note of your measurements, favorite silhouettes and colors. Photograph combinations you love so you can repeat them without guessing.
